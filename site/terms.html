3. Paid packs and licence keys

  • Some packs, like Fantasy Folk and Server Room, are unlocked with a licence key from us.
  • A key is for you personally. You can use it on your own computers, but please don't share, give away, sell or post your key publicly.
  • One key can hold several packs. When you buy another pack, we can upgrade your key and send you a new one that replaces the old one.

8. Your data

We collect as little as we can:

  • User count: the app sends an anonymous "+1" once a day so we can count users. No name, ID or files are sent, and you can turn it off in the tray menu.
  • Problem reports: if you use "Report a problem…", we only get what you type and, if you leave it ticked, basic app details like the version and your Windows version.
  • Accounts: we store your username, a scrambled copy of your password, the keys on your account, your badges and profile choices, and your Discord name and picture if you link Discord.

We use this only to run Bitty Buddies. We never sell it. Delete your account and its data goes with it.

9. Cookies and storage

This website doesn't use tracking, analytics or advertising cookies. It saves a few small things in your browser:

  • Your sign-in (essential): if you sign in to your account, we remember it in this browser so you stay signed in. It's removed when you sign out.
  • Your cookie choice (essential): so we don't ask you again on every page.
  • Advent doors (only if you press Accept): which doors of the advent calendar you've already opened.

You can change your mind at any time with , also at the bottom of every page. Choosing "Essential only" removes the advent door record.

To show the page, your browser also fetches things from other services, which see your connection's address like any website does: our fonts come from Google Fonts, the member count comes from Discord, and download and status information comes from GitHub and our own server. These don't set cookies through our site.
