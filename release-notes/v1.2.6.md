# 🧟 Bitty Buddies 1.2.6: Outbreak

## ✨ New: the Outbreak pack
Our biggest pack yet, with **42 buddies and builds** for a full zombie-apocalypse camp on your desktop.

- **9 zombies:** Shambler, Office Zombie, Crawler, Zombie Pup, Brute, Toxic Zombie, Zombie Chef, Zombie Clown and a flapping Zombie Crow.
- **Survivors:** Survivor, Hazmat, Field Medic, Tough Gran and the Scientist.
- **The army:** Soldier, Commander, Radio Operator, Army Engineer, Pilot and a brave Army Dog.
- **Vehicles:** Army Jeep, Army Truck, Tank, Field Ambulance, Helicopter and a twin-rotor **Chinook**.
- **16 outpost builds:** Watchtower, Barbed Wire, Sandbag Wall, Army Tent, Supply Crates, Barricade, Helipad, Floodlight Tower, Burning Barrel, Fuel Drums, Checkpoint, Radio Mast, Bunker, Wrecked Car, Graveyard and Field Hospital.

## 🧠 Zombies bite!
- Zombies hunt down other buddies (from any pack) and **bite them, turning them into zombies** who go hunting too.
- Bitten buddies **stay zombies until they're cured**. Only the **Field Medic**, the **Scientist** or the **Field Ambulance** can cure them.
- **Soldiers** and the **Army Dog** fend off zombies that come too close.
- Zombies get tangled in **Barbed Wire** and slow right down.
- The **Helicopter** and **Chinook** drop supply crates as they fly over.
- Right-click a zombie for **🧟 Bite someone**, or a medic for **💉 Cure a zombie**.
- New in **Play**: **🧟 Zombie outbreak!** and **📦 Supply drop!**

## 🩸 Gore toggle
**Play → 🩸 Gore** turns on blood splats when zombies bite and drippy trails behind them. It's **off by default**.

## 🔧 Fixes
- If a pack is taken off your licence key (for example after a refund), the app now removes just that pack and your other packs keep working.
