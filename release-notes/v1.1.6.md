## 👤 Bitty Buddies 1.1.6

### ✨ New
- **Sign in with your Bitty Buddies account.** Tray menu → **Sign in…** and use the same account as the website.
  - Your packs unlock straight away, and you stay signed in.
  - Packs you buy later unlock by themselves, no key to copy and paste.
  - Signing in is optional. Free pets work without an account, and **Enter licence key…** still works.
- **Report a problem…** in the tray. Tell us what went wrong in a little box and it comes straight to us.
- **Service status** in the tray, so you can check if something's down on our side.
