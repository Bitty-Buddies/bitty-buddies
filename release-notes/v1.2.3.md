# 🛒 Bitty Buddies 1.2.3: Packs in the Bitty Shop

## ✨ New
- **Packs in the Bitty Shop.** A new 🛒 **Packs** tab shows every pack you can buy (Builds, Fantasy Folk and the Everything Bundle), which ones you already own, and a Buy button that opens our shop in your browser. Open it from **🛒 Get more packs…** in the tray, or **Bitty Shop… → 🛒 Packs**.
- **Already bought one?** The Packs tab walks you through redeeming your code, with buttons for your account page and your licence keys.

## 🔑 Licence keys have moved
**Licence keys…** is no longer in the tray menu. You'll find it in **Bitty Shop… → 🛒 Packs → Licence keys…**, right next to everything else to do with packs. Signed in? Your packs still unlock by themselves, nothing to do.

## 🔧 Changes
- **Check for updates…** is gone from the tray. Bitty Buddies already checks every 30 minutes and downloads updates by itself, and **🎉 Restart to update** shows up when one is ready.
- Locked packs in the Spawn menu now say **Get this pack…** and take you straight to the Packs tab.
- Your account backup now remembers if you kept Builds free from before 1.2.0, so the Buddy Book on the website shows them as yours.
