# 🛒 Bitty Buddies 1.2.1

## ✨ New
- **Packs in the Bitty Shop.** A new 🛒 Packs tab shows every pack you can buy (Builds, Fantasy Folk and the Everything Bundle), which ones you already own, and a Buy button that opens our shop in your browser. Find it in the Bitty Shop or with **🛒 Get more packs…** in the tray.
- **Already bought one?** The Packs tab walks you through redeeming your code, with buttons for your account page, signing in and entering a licence key.

## 🔧 Changes
- Your account backup now remembers if you kept Builds free from before 1.2.0, so the Buddy Book on the website shows them as yours.
