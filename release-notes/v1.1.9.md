## 🎄 Bitty Buddies 1.1.9: Jolly Winter

### ✨ New
- **Jolly Winter advent calendar 🎄**, free for everyone. A new festive buddy or build opens every day from 1 to 25 December, with a very special visitor on Christmas Day. Missed a day? The doors you missed open when you're back, and every buddy is yours to keep.
- **Snow ❄️** falls on your desktop, drifts along the bottom of the screen and settles on top of your builds. It snows from December to 6 January, or pick **Snow** in the tray menu to have it always or never.
- **21 new buddies**:
  - **Critters:** Fennel the fox, Hoot the owl, Ribbit the frog, Prickles the hedgehog, Acorn the squirrel, Puddle the duckling and Shelly the snail.
  - **Fantasy Folk:** the Witch, Bard, Druid, Alchemist, Viking, Gnome and Healer. Already got Fantasy Folk? They're added to your pack for free.
  - **Builds:** a wishing well, a windmill, a park bench, a post box, a fountain, a market stall and a signpost.
- **Magic ✨**: the Witch flies around your screen on her broom, and the Wizard casts glowing magic balls that send other buddies boinging into the air. Right-click them to make it happen.
- **Hats and outfits 🎅**, starting with Santa hats. Put one on everyone from **Hats and outfits** in the tray, or right-click a pet to dress just that one.
- **New Year's Eve 🎆**: fireworks over your taskbar at midnight. Your buddies will want to celebrate.

### 🔧 Improvements
- Tighter security: the app only ever opens its own pages, your Bitty Buddies account and Discord sign-in, and never asks for your camera, microphone or other devices.
- Little fixes behind the scenes.
