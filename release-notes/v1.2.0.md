# 🪙 Bitty Buddies 1.2.0: Buddy Bucks and the Bitty Shop

## ✨ New
- **Buddy Bucks 🪙.** Your buddies earn coins just by being out on your desktop: one every couple of minutes, a little for naps, fetching the ball and winning battles, and a gift every day. You'll see little coins float up from them. Buddy Bucks are never sold for money.
- **The Bitty Shop 🛍️.** Spend Buddy Bucks on 8 new hats (party hat, beanie, flower crown, tiny crown, pirate hat, top hat, cowboy hat and wizard hat), 4 decorations (flower patch, picket fence, mailbox and lily pond) and 4 shop-only buddies: **Goblin, Bruno the bear, Frost the polar bear and Sparkle the unicorn**. Open it from the tray.
- **Buddy Book 📖.** See every buddy and build you've collected, plus your stats. Give your buddies names!
- **Friendship ❤.** The longer a buddy is out, the better friends you become. Pals hop when your cursor comes by, Best buddies learn a trick, Besties bring you a gift, and Forever friends get their own heart hat.
- **Hats for everyone.** Right-click a pet and pick from all the hats you own.

## 🔧 Changes
- **Builds is now a paid pack (£1).** Already had Bitty Buddies? You keep all your builds for free, forever.
- Earning gently slows down after a long day, so leaving your PC on all night isn't the "right" way to play.
- Signed in? Your Buddy Bucks, unlocks and friendships are backed up to your account.
